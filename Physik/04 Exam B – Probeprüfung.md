## Formulas
- Electric field: $E = \dfrac{F}{q}$  Field of a point charge: $E = k\dfrac{Q}{r^2}$
- Field of a long, straight wire: $B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$  Weight: $F_G = mg$
- Newton's second law: $F = ma$

**Constants:** $k = 8.99\cdot10^{9}$ N m²/C², $\mu_0 = 4\pi\cdot10^{-7}$ T m/A, $e = 1.602\cdot10^{-19}$ C, $m_e = 9.109\cdot10^{-31}$ kg, $g = 9.81$ m/s²

**Directions:** up, down, left and right as drawn on the page. ⊙ = out of the page (towards you), ⊗ = into the page (away from you).

**Scoring:** no half points. A numerical result earns its point only with a correct unit. Direction answers need a short justification where asked. A correct method applied to a wrong earlier result still earns its points.

## 1. Short questions
**(a) (3 points)** A test charge q = +2.0 nC placed at point P experiences a force of 6.0·10⁻⁵ N to the right. (i) What is the electric field at P (magnitude and direction)? (ii) What force would a charge of −5.0 nC experience at P? (iii) What is the field at P after the test charge has been removed?

**(b) (3 points)** Convert: (i) 0.45 mT into µT, (ii) 3.2·10⁵ N/C into kN/C, (iii) 1.2·10⁴ nC into mC.

**(c) (3 points)** A loop of wire that carries a current acts as a magnet (an electromagnet). What happens to its magnetic field if (i) the current is increased, (ii) the current is reversed? (iii) Name one advantage of such an electromagnet compared to a permanent magnet.

## 2. Field lines
**(a) (4 points)** Sketch the electric field lines (with direction) of (i) a single negative point charge and (ii) a positive and a negative charge of equal magnitude.

**(b) (3 points)** Two bar magnets lie side by side, with opposite orientation (see sketch). Sketch the magnetic field lines (with direction). Where is the field strongest?

```
   N        S
   
   S        N
```

## 3. Two charges
Two positive charges lie on a line: Q₁ = +9.0 µC at x = 0 and Q₂ = +1.0 µC at x = 20 cm.

**(a) (3 points)** Find the magnitude and direction of the electric field at the midpoint M (x = 10 cm).

**(b) (3 points)** At which point on the x-axis is the electric field zero?

## 4. Electron (5 points)
An electron is in a uniform electric field of 500 N/C that points up. Calculate the electric force on the electron (magnitude and direction) and compare it with its weight. What is the acceleration of the electron?

## 5. Compasses around a wire (4 points)
A long, straight wire is perpendicular to the page and carries a current **out of the page**. Four compasses are placed around it (neglect the Earth's magnetic field). Draw the direction in which the N pole of each compass points. What changes if the current is reversed?

         1

   3     ⊙     4

         2

## 6. Power line (5 points)
A long, horizontal power line in the plane of the page carries a current of 200 A to the right. (a) At what distance from the line is its magnetic field as strong as the Earth's magnetic field (≈ 50 µT)? (b) In which direction does the line's field point at a point below the line?

## 7. Two wires
Two long, straight wires are perpendicular to the page and 10 cm apart. Both currents point out of the page: I₁ = 30 A in the left wire, I₂ = 10 A in the right wire.

```
   I₁ ⊙          M          ⊙ I₂
       |←──── 10 cm ────→|
```

**(a) (4 points)** Find the magnitude and direction of the magnetic field at the midpoint M.

**(b) (3 points)** At which point between the wires is the magnetic field zero?
